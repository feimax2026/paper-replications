# Descriptive results: Time-extension analysis: FY2020–FY2024

This is a descriptive checkpoint, not yet an estimate of the paper's state-switching model.

## What the public-data panel shows

- Mean buy bid volume changed from 21.444 million kWh in FY2020 to 19.529 million kWh in FY2024.
- Mean sell bid volume increased from 21.831 million kWh to 24.509 million kWh over the same period.
- Mean buy-minus-sell tightness moved from -0.387 million kWh to -4.980 million kWh.
- Mean prices do not move monotonically across fiscal years. That motivates a nonlinear, regime-dependent model rather than a before/after mean comparison.

## Interpretation boundary

These figures describe market evolution; they do not establish causality or reproduce the paper's parameter estimates. The temperature column is a public daily max/min midpoint proxy, so it must be replaced or stress-tested before making an exact model-level claim.
